# Nacre

Open `/projects/viz/` on the [local preview server](../../AGENTS.md). Begin listening on desktop or choose Enter VR on Quest. Pause or resume with the desktop button or either controller trigger. Each load draws a random unsigned 32-bit seed and displays it in the Seed field. Enter a number from 0 through 4294967295 and choose Use seed to reopen the chamber at the beginning, then begin playback. A direct link with `?seed=123` also selects a composition once. Refresh draws a fresh seed.

A single sculpture with 43 ribbon layers sits at seated eye level, 6.5 m ahead at 1.022× model scale. It sways and tilts gently on the musical clock. A single GPU point draw animates 480 motes: kick hits send an expanding spray from the rim, while bell notes light a winding thread at their pitch height. Scheduled kick onsets expand every form. Bell pitch selects a band of lips to lift and illuminate for the note envelope. Smoothed post-compressor RMS opens the geometry and brightens its rim. A dark star field remains fixed for orientation.

- [Score](score.mjs) derives eight-bar phrases from an integer seed and phrase number. Each seed chooses a home tonic and major/minor mode. Four eight-bar periods visit the relative key and return home. Questions end on V, and answers select among authentic, deceptive and plagal arrivals. Minor-key V uses a raised leading tone. Each lead rests on active triad tones, with register and previous pitch guiding voice movement and optional stepwise pickups. Adjacent 32-bar subjects inherit half their contour, while rhythms, orchestration and harmonic paths vary. Long bell notes retain a softer body before their release. See [period form](https://musictheory.pugetsound.edu/mt21c/PeriodForm.html) and [harmonic rhythm](https://musictheory.pugetsound.edu/mt21c/HarmonicRhythm.html).
- [Audio](audio.mjs) schedules sixteenth notes against AudioContext time with 160 ms lookahead. The four sustained pad voices use half gain (−6 dB) before the shared delay. Voices use synthesized noise, oscillators, FM and a shared stereo delay. Suspended audio freezes the transport. After a timer stall, scheduling skips missed onsets. Finished voices disconnect and the scheduler retains only the current phrase.
- [Chamber](chamber.mjs) deforms instanced strips in a vertex shader. Each seven-period movement opens as a shell and visits six forms in seeded order: a spiral, tide, trefoil knot, folded globe, paired wings and hourglass. Geometry, folds, winding and color interpolate over five beats at section changes. [Application](app.mjs) uses the same audio clock for rendering and the shared XRFrameState helper to gate untracked frames.

## Validation

Desktop Chrome verified playback start, pause and resume, all seven sculpture forms and transitions, and distinct particle responses to a kick and two different bell pitches. The render check confirmed the 1.022× scale and 480-particle buffer. An offline Web Audio check synthesized events from each arrangement. A 32-bar mix comparison verified that pad voice gain was halved across all four arrangements.

Seed 123 produced 75,280 finite events across 512 periods. Across 48 seeds and 32 periods per seed, all 34,118 melodic landing notes belonged to their active triads and 6,125 pickups approached their target within three semitones.

On Quest, replay seed 123 and listen through seven eight-bar periods to see every form. Check the pad balance, seated framing, particle visibility in stereo, both trigger controls and sustained frame cadence. Physical headset validation is pending.
