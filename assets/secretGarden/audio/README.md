## Bee buzz

`bee-buzz.wav`: derived from seconds 6–9 of “Bee buzzing.wav” by DrDufus.
Source: https://freesound.org/people/DrDufus/sounds/462875/
Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/462/462875_4474247-hq.mp3
License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Retrieved 2026-11-08. Decoded high-quality MP3 preview to mono PCM, extracted 6–9 seconds, Applied a zero-phase 120 Hz high-pass to reduce a recurring low-frequency source transient, then repeated the excerpt with 300 ms equal-power crossfades and a matching loop-boundary crossfade to produce a five-second WAV. RMS matched to the preceding asset with peak ceiling .95. Natural pitch and speed retained.
